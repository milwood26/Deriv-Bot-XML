Legal Requirements

The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 requires all non-domestic premises to have appropriate fire detection and warning systems. Failure to comply can result in severe penalties, including unlimited fines and imprisonment.

Our expert team ensures your fire alarm systems meet all legal obligations, keeping your business compliant and your occupants safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should fire alarms be tested?

Fire alarms should be tested weekly by pressing a manual call point. A full service and inspection by a competent person should be carried out at least every 6 months for most premises, as recommended by BS 5839.

What type of fire alarm system do I need?

The type of system depends on your property size, usage, and risk assessment. We provide free surveys to recommend the most suitable system—whether conventional, addressable, wireless, or interconnected domestic alarms.

Are you BS 5839 compliant?

Yes, all our fire alarm installations and maintenance services fully comply with BS 5839, the British Standard for fire detection and fire alarm systems in buildings.

Do you provide fire alarm certificates?

Yes, we issue full certification upon installation and after each service visit, documenting compliance and any recommendations for improvements.

What areas do you cover?

We provide fire alarm services across the entire United Kingdom, including England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.

Can you upgrade existing fire alarm systems?

Absolutely. We can upgrade outdated systems to meet current standards, integrate new technology, and expand existing installations as your building requirements change.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes, we offer flexible maintenance contracts with various service frequencies to suit your needs and budget, ensuring ongoing compliance and system reliability.

What happens if my fire alarm keeps going off?

False alarms can be caused by various issues including dust, steam, or faulty detectors. We offer false alarm investigation services to identify and resolve the root cause.
