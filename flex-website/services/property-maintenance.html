Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum charge?

Our minimum charge is one hour at our standard hourly rate. There's no call-out fee, so you only pay for the actual work time plus materials.

Can you handle multiple different jobs in one visit?

Absolutely! That's what we specialise in. Bring us your 'to-do' list and we'll tackle as many jobs as possible in the booked time slot.

Do you supply materials?

Yes, we can source and supply all necessary materials at cost price with no markup. Alternatively, you can purchase materials yourself if you prefer.

Are you insured for all types of work?

Yes, we carry full public liability insurance covering all aspects of property maintenance work. We can provide certificate copies on request.

Do you offer regular maintenance contracts?

We offer scheduled maintenance plans for landlords, businesses, and homeowners who want regular upkeep. Contact us for customised contract options.

What areas do you cover?

We provide property maintenance services across the entire United Kingdom. Our network ensures local coverage wherever you're located.

Can you work around my schedule?

We offer flexible scheduling including early mornings, evenings, and weekends to minimise disruption to your home or business operations.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

Your satisfaction is guaranteed. If you're not happy with any aspect of our work, we'll return and rectify it at no additional cost.
