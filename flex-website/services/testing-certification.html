Frequently Asked Questions

How often should fire alarms be tested?

Fire alarms should be tested weekly using a manual call point. Monthly inspections and quarterly professional testing are also recommended. Annual comprehensive testing by a qualified engineer is a legal requirement for most commercial properties.

What happens if my system fails testing?

If any component fails testing, we'll provide a detailed report identifying the issue and recommend remedial actions. We can often carry out repairs immediately or schedule a follow-up visit to rectify the problem.

Do I need certification for residential properties?

While private homes don't legally require certification, landlords must ensure fire safety systems are properly maintained. Commercial properties, HMOs, and businesses must have regular testing and valid certificates.

How long does testing take?

Testing duration depends on system size. A small system might take 1-2 hours, while larger commercial installations could take a full day. We'll provide an estimated timeframe when booking.

What standards do you test to?

We test fire alarms to BS 5839 standards and emergency lighting to BS 5266. These British Standards ensure your systems meet all legal requirements and industry best practices.

Can you test systems you didn't install?

Yes, we test and certify all makes and models of fire safety systems, regardless of who installed them. We'll assess the current condition and ensure compliance.

What documentation will I receive?

You'll receive digital certificates, detailed test reports, maintenance logs, and compliance documentation. All documents are stored in your customer portal for easy access.

Do you offer emergency testing services?

Yes, we provide emergency testing and certification for properties requiring immediate compliance documentation. Contact our emergency line for urgent requirements.
