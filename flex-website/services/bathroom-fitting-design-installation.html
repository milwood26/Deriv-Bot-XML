Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical bathroom installation take?

A standard bathroom installation typically takes 7-10 working days, depending on the complexity and any structural work required. Wet rooms may take 10-14 days due to additional waterproofing requirements.

Do I need to be home during the installation?

We prefer to have access to your property, but you don't need to be present all the time. We'll need to consult with you on key decisions and for the final handover inspection.

Can you work with bathrooms I've already purchased?

Yes, we can install customer-supplied fixtures. However, we recommend consulting with us before purchase to ensure compatibility and optimal layout for your space.

What guarantees do you offer?

All our installations come with a minimum 5-year guarantee on workmanship. Manufacturer warranties apply to fixtures and fittings. Extended guarantees are available for premium packages.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical work?

Yes, our team includes qualified plumbers and Part P certified electricians, so all aspects of your bathroom installation are covered under one service.

Can you create accessible bathrooms?

Absolutely. We specialise in designing and installing accessible bathrooms including walk-in showers, grab rails, raised toilets, and wheelchair-accessible layouts.

What about ventilation?

Proper ventilation is essential. We install extractor fans as standard and can advise on additional ventilation solutions to prevent condensation and mould.

Do you offer finance options?

We can discuss flexible payment plans for larger projects. Contact us to explore finance options that suit your budget.
