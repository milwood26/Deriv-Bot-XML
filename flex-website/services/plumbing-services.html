Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to emergencies?

For emergency calls, we aim to have a plumber with you within 2 hours during business hours, and within 4 hours for out-of-hours emergencies. We prioritise based on severity.

Are your plumbers qualified and insured?

Yes, all our plumbers are fully qualified, Gas Safe registered where applicable, and carry public liability insurance. We only employ experienced, vetted professionals.

Do you charge for call-outs?

We charge a standard call-out fee which includes diagnosis and the first portion of labour. This fee is waived if you proceed with recommended repairs over a certain value.

What areas do you cover?

We provide plumbing services across the entire United Kingdom. Our network of plumbers ensures local coverage with quick response times wherever you're located.

Do you offer guarantees on your work?

Absolutely. All our plumbing work comes with a minimum 90-day guarantee on labour. Parts carry manufacturer warranties, typically 1-5 years depending on the item.

Can you work with my home insurance?

Yes, we work with all major insurance companies. We can provide detailed reports, invoices, and documentation required for insurance claims related to plumbing damage.

Do you install customer-supplied fixtures?

We can install customer-supplied items, though we recommend consulting with us first to ensure compatibility. Note that manufacturer warranties may not apply to customer-supplied fixtures.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, and bank transfers. For larger jobs, we may require a deposit with the balance due on completion.
